Biography

Ben Evans has been a working technologist for almost 30 years, with experience developing and running world-class projects in a number of sectors including finance, gaming, media and third-sector.

Career

He is currently Observability Lead and Senior Principal Software Engineer at IBM / Red Hat Runtimes. Previously he was Lead Architect for Instrumentation at New Relic, and co-founded jClarity, a performance tools startup. He has also worked as Chief Architect for Listed Derivatives at Deutsche Bank and as Senior Technical Instructor for Morgan Stanley.

Ben has worked on a wide variety of projects, including the Google IPO, websites for some of the biggest movies of the 90s, low-latency trading systems as well as founding a machine learning startup (jClarity) in 2012, which was a participant in the Horizon 2020 project SeReCa -- to research technologies for Secure Reactive Cloud Environments for sovereign hosting in the EU. jClarity was acquired by Microsoft in 2019.

Speaking, Writing & F/OSS

He is a regular speaker at conferences and events around the world, including Devoxx, JNation, Oracle Code One, QCon, JAX and many others. He has also written for a number of publications including InfoQ, The BBC and The New Stack.

Ben is a Java Champion, a former member of the Java Community Process Executive Committee. He is an award-winning speaker at technical conferences worldwide and a community leader in the Java / JVM worlds. He was made a Java Champion in 2013, and has won the Rock Star Speaker award 3 times.

Ben is the author of eight books, including the best-selling The Well-Grounded Java Developer.

His technical articles are read by thousands of developers every month. Ben is a regular speaker and educator on topics such as the Java platform, Observability, systems architecture, performance and concurrency for companies and conferences all over the world.

Ben has been involved in contributing to F/OSS since the late 90s. His most recent contributions have been to standards and code for Observability -- including OpenTelemetry, the defacto OSS standard for Observability data. Ben has solid experience sitting on Internet standards bodies (including the Java Community Process Executive Committee) and in working closely with lawyers (e.g. when open-sourcing $600M of intellectual property during his time at New Relic).

Mentoring, Teaching & Educating

Teaching, mentoring and educating others has been a part of his working life from the very beginning. He spent 4 years working as an independent consultant and educator for a range of clients - including Morgan Stanley, where he was a senior instructor working to create and deliver courses for the bank's technical staff, including for their award-winning graduate hires programme. He spent 3 seasons teaching in India & 3 in New York, as well as shorter stints in China, Japan and Hungary.
