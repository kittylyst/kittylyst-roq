I'll be appearing at LJC Winter Unconf 2026 at the IBM office in London.

  

If you haven't been to an unconference before, then it's a little different from the traditional session-based approach where a single speaker stands up and delivers a pre-prepared talk. Instead, participants group together around a shared interest for each session and learn from each other.

  

They are frequently one of my favorite sorts of events to attend, and the LJC has been putting on these types of events for over 15 years. This year's Keynote Speaker is my colleague and friend, Holly Cummins.

  

LJC Winter Unconf 2026 runs on December 4, 2026. Check the conference site for details and I hope to see you there!
